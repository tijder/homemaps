# In the car

The same navigation on the car's screen. Your phone stays in charge: it plans
the route, follows you and speaks the instructions; the car shows the map and
the panel.

On the car's screen you get:

- **Where to?** with **Home**, **Work** and your **Recent places**, and a
  search. Set home and work in the app on the phone, on the card of a place.
- A route preview with the alternatives (**Routes**), and **Start**.
- Turn-by-turn: the next manoeuvre, the lanes, the exit sign with its number
  and road shields, the speed limit and your speed, the speed camera ahead, the
  overhead signs, and the arrival time.

The panel is the app's blue with white icons, and the map follows the dark
mode of your phone. Distances read as on the phone ("1.5 km").

If the car says "Open HomeMaps on your phone and turn on location", the
phone has no location yet: unlock it once and allow location.

## Android Auto

HomeMaps appears among the navigation apps in Android Auto once it is
installed on the phone. Head units only accept apps that came from Google
Play; for an APK that you installed yourself, turn on **Unknown sources** in
the developer settings of the Android Auto app on the phone (tap the version
number ten times to unlock them).

## Apple CarPlay

HomeMaps is a CarPlay navigation app. Plug in the phone or connect
wirelessly and the app is on the CarPlay home screen. Before iOS 18 the lanes
are drawn in the top bar; from iOS 18 CarPlay shows them in the panel itself.
