# Settings

**Map and route**

- **Map**: the style (Map, Light, Dark), day and night, and the layers Traffic
  and Speed cameras. See [Map and search](map-and-search.md).
- **Transport**: the means of transport a new route starts with.
- **Places**: home, work and recent places.

**Sharing**

- **Dawarich**: your Dawarich server, sign in, family, recording while
  navigating.
- **Location sharing**: send your position to your own server while
  navigating.

**App**

- **Server** (phone apps only): the address of your HomeMaps installation.
  The app checks whether the map, search and routes work there.
- **About**: the version, the sources of the data (OpenStreetMap, NDW) and the
  software (MapLibre, Valhalla, Photon), and the licences. **Save log** writes
  the app's log to a file for troubleshooting.

The language follows your device: English or Dutch.
